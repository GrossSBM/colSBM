# A collection of 8 food webs

A dataset containing 8 stream food webs coming from the same study.

## Usage

``` r
foodwebs
```

## Format

A named list of 8 binary matrices:

- M_Martins:

  105 species and 343 feeding interactions

- NC_Cooper:

  58 species and 126 feeding interactions

- NC_Herlzier:

  71 species and 148 feeding interactions

- NZ_Venlaw:

  69 species and 187 feeding interactions

- NZ_Berwick:

  79 species and 240 feeding interactions

- NZ_North_Col:

  78 species and 241 feeding interactions

- NZ_Powder:

  78 species and 268 feeding interactions

- NZ_Trib:

  98 species and 629 feeding interactions

## Source

<http://www.web-of-life.es/>

## Details

Reference: Thompson, R. M. and Townsend, C. R. (2003), Impacts on stream
food webs of native and exotic forest: an intercontinental comparison.
Ecology 84(1): 145-161
